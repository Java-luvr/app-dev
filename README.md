## Drawing Closer (2024)

![a movie](https://resizing.flixster.com/yD7sSXrp40OY36ZaH-Z_KmaS7jw=/620x336/v2/https://resizing.flixster.com/-XZAfHZM39UwaGJIFWKAE8fS0ak=/v3/t/assets/p27270429_i_h10_aa.jpg)

<p>Adapted from Ao Morita’s poignant novel, Drawing Closer follows Akito Hayasaka, a teenage boy diagnosed with a terminal illness and given one year to live. His world shifts when he meets Haruna Sakurai, a girl whose own prognosis is even more dire—only six months. Despite their ticking clocks, the two form a bond that transcends time, illness, and despair.</p>

<p>Akito, knowing Haruna’s time is shorter, devotes himself to making her final months vibrant and meaningful. Through shared experiences, laughter, and quiet companionship, he helps her rediscover joy and hope. Their relationship blossoms into a tender romance, not built on grand gestures, but on the everyday magic of simply being together.</p>
